How PaperTrail works

When a bill, invoice or receipt arrives from another organisation, someone usually has to retype it and check it against another document by hand. PaperTrail does that first pass for them, then hands the decision to a person.

📄

1. Read

An AI model looks at each document, a photo, scan or PDF, and writes down every detail: names, dates, items, amounts. If something is unreadable, it says so instead of guessing.

🧮

2. Check each document

Simple, reliable rules verify each document on its own: do the totals add up? Is the GST number genuine? Is the medicine name real?

🔗

3. Compare the two

The documents are checked against each other: same patient? Was every billed item actually ordered or prescribed? AI only helps when names differ, like a brand vs its generic.

⚖️

4. Recommend

You get one of three verdicts, approve, double-check or stop, with the exact reasons. A person always makes the final call.

Why this is a useful AI project

  • Never guesses: every value comes with how sure the AI was, and where on the page it read it.
  • Explainable: every verdict points at the exact field that caused it.
  • Measured: it was tested on 120 document pairs with known problems planted in them (see How accurate is it?).
  • Extensible: a new kind of paperwork is a small plug-in, not a rewrite.

How accurate is it?

We generated 120 realistic document pairs (60 insurance claims, 60 supplier invoices) as clean images, office scans and badly damaged scans. In half of them we deliberately planted a problem, then measured how often PaperTrail noticed. These numbers are read straight from the saved test results.
